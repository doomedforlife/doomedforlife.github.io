History of Pipa

The Pipa(Chinese:琵琶) is a traditional Chinese plucking string instrument. It is shaped like a pear with long neck and has four strings.
It has a range of over three octaves and is played by plucking the strings with fake fingernails(or actual fingernails if they are strong enough, just pray they don't snap :D).

The term "Pipa" originally referred to the circular shaped instrument with long neck, which is the "Qin Pipa"(Chinese:秦琵琶) during The Qin dynasty.
