Pipa

History of Pipa

The Pipa is a traditional Chinese plucking string instrument. It is shaped like a pear with long neck and has four strings.

How to play a Pipa

Here are some basic techniques for playing the Pipa:

  • Hold the Pipa properly with your left hand
  • Use your right hand to pluck the strings
